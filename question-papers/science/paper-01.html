Science — Mock Paper 1

Three parts, one paper: Part I is Physics, Part II is Chemistry, Part III is Biology. Each part is self-contained — attempt them in any order, but keep to a single 2½-hour sitting for the full paper.

Maximum Marks: 80 Time Allowed: 2½ Hours Part I: 27 · Part II: 27 · Part III: 26
1 General Instructions 2 Part I — Physics 3 Part II — Chemistry 4 Part III — Biology 5 Answer Key

1. General Instructions

  • Answers to this paper must be written on separate answer sheets.
  • You will not be allowed to write during the first 15 minutes — this time is to be spent reading the question paper.
  • All three parts are compulsory. Attempt every question within each part.
  • The intended marks for questions or parts of questions are given in brackets [ ].
  • Balanced chemical equations must show correct symbols and states where relevant.
This combines three board subjects into one practice paper

CISCE conducts Physics, Chemistry and Biology as three separate 80-mark papers. This mock combines all three into a single integrated paper for revision purposes — it is not sourced from, endorsed by, or affiliated with CISCE.

2. Part I — Physics (27 Marks)

Question 1 [7]

  1. State the SI unit of power. [1]
  2. Define the "moment of a force". [1]
  3. A machine has a velocity ratio of 4 and an efficiency of 80%. Find its mechanical advantage. [2]
  4. State one factor on which the refractive index of a medium depends. [1]
  5. Name the phenomenon responsible for the twinkling of stars. [1]
  6. Write the relation between the frequency (f) and time period (T) of a wave. [1]

Question 2 [10] — Work, Energy & Power

A body of mass 5 kg is lifted vertically through a height of 4 m in 2 seconds. Take g = 10 m/s².

  1. Find the work done against gravity. [2]
  2. Find the power developed. [2]
  3. Find the potential energy gained by the body at the top. [2]
  4. If the body is now allowed to fall freely from that height, find its velocity just before it hits the ground. [2]
  5. Find its kinetic energy just before hitting the ground, and verify that it equals the potential energy found in (iii). [2]

Question 3 [10] — Current Electricity

A 4 Ω resistor and a 6 Ω resistor are connected to a 12 V battery.

  1. Find the total resistance if they are connected in series. [2]
  2. Find the current drawn from the battery in this series arrangement. [2]
  3. Find the total resistance if they are instead connected in parallel. [3]
  4. Find the current drawn from the battery in this parallel arrangement. [3]

3. Part II — Chemistry (27 Marks)

Question 4 [7]

  1. Write the molecular formula of ethanoic acid. [1]
  2. Name the gas evolved when dilute hydrochloric acid reacts with sodium carbonate. [1]
  3. State Faraday's First Law of Electrolysis. [2]
  4. Name the product formed when ethanol undergoes dehydration in the presence of hot, concentrated sulphuric acid at 170°C. [1]
  5. Which gas is produced at the cathode during the electrolysis of acidified water? [1]
  6. Define the term "alloy". [1]

Question 5 [10] — Mole Concept

Given: molar mass of CO₂ = 44 g/mol, molar mass of H₂O = 18 g/mol, molar volume at STP = 22.4 L, Avogadro's number = 6.022 × 10²³.

  1. Find the number of moles in 22 g of CO₂. [2]
  2. Find the number of molecules in that quantity of CO₂. [2]
  3. Find the volume this quantity of CO₂ would occupy at STP. [2]
  4. Find the mass of 3 moles of water. [2]
  5. Find the number of moles of oxygen atoms present in 3 moles of water. [2]

Question 6 [10] — Reactions

  1. Write balanced chemical equations for: (i) zinc reacting with dilute sulphuric acid; (ii) sodium hydroxide reacting with ammonium chloride on heating; (iii) the thermal decomposition of calcium carbonate. [6]
  2. A colourless gas turns lime water milky. Identify the gas and write the balanced equation for the reaction that causes this. [4]

4. Part III — Biology (26 Marks)

Question 7 [6]

  1. Name the functional unit of the kidney. [1]
  2. Define "photoperiodism". [1]
  3. Name the hormone secreted by the islets of Langerhans that lowers blood sugar. [1]
  4. Name the process by which a male and a female gamete fuse. [1]
  5. Name the pigment primarily responsible for photosynthesis. [1]
  6. State one function of the placenta. [1]

Question 8 [10] — The Neuron & Reflex Action

  1. Name the three main parts of a neuron and state one function of each. [6]
  2. Distinguish between a reflex action and a voluntary action. [4]

Question 9 [10] — Genetics

A pure tall pea plant (TT) is crossed with a pure dwarf pea plant (tt). Tallness (T) is dominant over dwarfism (t).

  1. Write the genotype and phenotype of the F1 generation. [2]
  2. Write the genotypic and phenotypic ratio of the F2 generation obtained by self-pollinating F1. [4]
  3. If 400 F2 plants are obtained, how many would be expected to be dwarf? [2]
  4. State Mendel's Law of Dominance. [2]

5. Answer Key

Attempt the full paper under a real timer before expanding these.

✓

Part I — Question 1

(a) Watt. (b) The moment of a force is the product of the force and the perpendicular distance from the pivot to the line of action of the force. (c) M.A. = efficiency × V.R. = 0.8 × 4 = 3.2. (d) The refractive index depends on the wavelength (colour) of light used (also accept: the nature of the two media). (e) Atmospheric refraction. (f) f = 1/T.

✓

Part I — Question 2 (Work, Energy & Power)

(i) W = mgh = 5×10×4 = 200 J. (ii) P = W/t = 200/2 = 100 W. (iii) PE = mgh = 200 J. (iv) v² = 2gh = 2×10×4 = 80 → v = √80 ≈ 8.94 m/s. (v) KE = ½mv² = 0.5×5×80 = 200 J — matches the PE found in (iii), confirming energy conservation.

✓

Part I — Question 3 (Current Electricity)

(i) Series R = 4+6 = 10 Ω. (ii) I = V/R = 12/10 = 1.2 A. (iii) Parallel: 1/R = 1/4+1/6 = 5/12 → R = 12/5 = 2.4 Ω. (iv) I = V/R = 12/2.4 = 5 A.

✓

Part II — Question 4

(a) CH₃COOH. (b) Carbon dioxide (CO₂). (c) The mass of a substance deposited or liberated at an electrode is directly proportional to the quantity of charge (current × time) passed through the electrolyte. (d) Ethene (ethylene, C₂H₄) — water is eliminated. (e) Hydrogen. (f) An alloy is a homogeneous mixture of two or more metals (or a metal and a non-metal), made to combine desirable properties not found in the pure metal alone.

✓

Part II — Question 5 (Mole Concept)

(i) 22/44 = 0.5 mol. (ii) 0.5 × 6.022×10²³ = 3.011×10²³ molecules. (iii) 0.5 × 22.4 = 11.2 L. (iv) 3 × 18 = 54 g. (v) each H₂O molecule contains one oxygen atom, so 3 mol water contains 3 mol oxygen atoms.

✓

Part II — Question 6 (Reactions)

(a)(i) Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂. (ii) NaOH + NH₄Cl →(heat) NaCl + NH₃ + H₂O. (iii) CaCO₃ →(heat) CaO + CO₂.

(b) The gas is carbon dioxide. CO₂ + Ca(OH)₂ → CaCO₃↓ (white precipitate, causing the milkiness) + H₂O.

✓

Part III — Question 7

(a) Nephron. (b) Photoperiodism is a plant's response to the relative lengths of day and night, especially affecting flowering. (c) Insulin. (d) Fertilization. (e) Chlorophyll. (f) The placenta allows exchange of nutrients, oxygen and waste products between mother and foetus (any one correct function).

✓

Part III — Question 8 (Neuron & Reflex Action)

(i) Cyton (cell body): contains the nucleus and controls the metabolic activities of the neuron. Dendrites: receive nerve impulses from other neurons or receptors. Axon: transmits nerve impulses away from the cell body to the next neuron or an effector.

(ii) A reflex action is a rapid, involuntary response controlled by the spinal cord without conscious thought. A voluntary action is a deliberate, conscious response controlled by the cerebrum (brain).

✓

Part III — Question 9 (Genetics)

(i) F1 genotype = Tt (all offspring); phenotype = all Tall (T is dominant).

(ii) F2 genotypic ratio = 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt. F2 phenotypic ratio = 3 Tall : 1 Dwarf.

(iii) Dwarf fraction = 1/4 of 400 = 100 dwarf plants.

(iv) Law of Dominance: when two contrasting alleles (factors) for a trait are present together in an organism, only one of them (the dominant allele) expresses itself in the phenotype, while the other (recessive allele) remains masked.
